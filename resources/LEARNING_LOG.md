# Day 1 — Product Definition

## What problem are we solving?

People often have goals but struggle to turn those goals into clear actions.

Plan2Action helps turn:

> "What do I want to achieve?"

into:

> "What should I do next?"

## What I learned

- A product should solve a problem.
- A feature should exist for a reason.
- A product requirement describes what the product needs to do.
- MVP means the smallest useful version of a product.

## Questions

- What makes a problem worth solving?
- How do I distinguish a feature from a product requirement?
